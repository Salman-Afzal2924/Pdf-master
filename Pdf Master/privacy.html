Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

At PDFMaster, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit and use our website.

1. PDF File Processing

Our PDF tools are designed to process uploaded files directly in your web browser. In the current implementation, files are not intentionally uploaded to a PDFMaster server.

However, external libraries and services may be loaded from third-party providers. Please avoid using this website for documents containing highly confidential information.

2. Personal Information

PDFMaster does not currently require visitors to create accounts to use its PDF tools. If you contact us, the information you provide may be used to respond to your message.

3. Cookies and Advertising

PDFMaster may use third-party services, including advertising providers such as Google AdSense, in the future.

These services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide advertisements, measure performance and improve services, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party JavaScript libraries and services to provide PDF functionality. These providers may have their own privacy practices and policies.

5. Data Security

We aim to provide a safe and reliable experience. However, no website or online service can guarantee complete security.

6. Children's Privacy

PDFMaster is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our website and services develop. Any updates will be published on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
